Historical Chronicles & Migration Routes

Select any year (1796–2026) or search an Italian town to inspect its sovereign regime, historical province, and State Archive.

    Provinces are color-coded by their ruling sovereign regime on 1 July of the selected year.

    Toggle emigration ports and trunk rail corridors (1839–1920) to trace how ancestors traveled from inland comuni to the coast.

    Press / to search. Arrow keys change the year by 1. Shift plus an arrow changes the year by 10. Space plays the years. Esc closes the card.
